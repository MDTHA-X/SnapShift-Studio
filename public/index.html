How to Successfully Replace Your SSD Without Losing Anything

Follow these 5 simple stages to swap your drive and restore your entire workspace seamlessly.

1

Capture Snapshot to Internal HDD (Current Step)

Use SnapShift Studio right now to take a complete snapshot of all files, configurations, and software lists onto drive D: or F:. This preserves all your personal data completely outside the SSD.

Ready in SnapShift Studio
2

Prepare Windows 11 Bootable USB Media

Insert a spare USB flash drive (8GB+) and download Microsoft's official Media Creation Tool to make an official Windows 11 installation drive.

Official Microsoft Media Creation Tool →
3

Physically Swap the SSD Inside Your PC

Turn off the PC, disconnect the power cable. Remove the old PNY 128GB SSD and insert your new SSD into the slot. Crucial: Keep the internal Western Digital HDD connected! It safely holds your snapshot files.

4

Install Clean Windows on the New SSD

Boot your PC with the Windows installation USB. Select the new SSD as the install destination. Complete the initial Windows account setup.

5

Restore Everything with 1-Click from HDD!

Open File Explorer, go to your HDD (D:\SnapShift_Snapshots\...), and double-click RESTORE_ALL_FILES.bat. It restores all your files, developer keys, environment variables, and auto-installs your apps!

Zero technical effort required
